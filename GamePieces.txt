Aiden Zeigler, Nick Terrill
LO6A KerPlop Game

Implements Drawable (No interaction, doesn't move):
    - Rock (R)

Extends GamePiece, Implements Moveable:
    - Guard (G)
        - Will move in the player's direction every other move.
        - Will do interaction type KILL once the guard is in the same spot as the player.
    - Lawnmower (L)
        - Will choose to move left, stay in the same spot, or move right randomly.
        - Will do interaction type HIT once the Lawnmower is in the same spot as the player.

Extends GamePiece:
    - Wizard (W)
        - Will do interaction type KILL once the player is EXACTLY 3 spots away from the player, 
        unless there is a game piece in between.
    - Banana Peel (B)
        - Will do interaction type HIT when the player steps on the same square as the banana peel, otherwise it 
        will do nothing.
    - Treasure (T)
        - When the player stands on this space, they get a point.
    - Elevator (E)
        - When the player lands on this space, they are advanced to the next level.
